# QAF artwork

The portrait poster at the repository root is the visual README. Its glass Bloch sphere and two readouts provide a conceptual illustration of the project's state and probe interface. The three miniature instruments depict preparation, interference and measurement. The histogram on the glass is decorative. The numerical experiments are recorded in the evidence directory.

The landscape companion appears in the research portfolio. Both pieces were generated in November 2026 for this project. The portrait follows the visual design established in Brian Mingus's [Decision Programs](https://github.com/mingusb/decision-programs) and [OpenSSH-Lean](https://github.com/mingusb/openssh-lean) READMEs. The companion uses the same subject in the format of the [research gallery](https://brianmingus.com/).
